## Mandatory assertion validation

A relying party must validate all applicable items below:

SIGNATURE_VALIDATION=REQUIRED

AUTH_TIME_PRESENCE=REQUIRED  
CNF_JKT_PRESENCE_WHERE_SENDER_CONSTRAINT_REQUIRED=REQUIRED  
SENDER_PROOF_BINDING=REQUIRED  
ENVIRONMENT_VALIDATION=REQUIRED  
FAILURE_MODE=DENY

UNKNOWN_KID=DENY  
WILDCARD_AUDIENCE=DENY  
ALG_NONE=DENY  
TOKEN_SUPPLIED_JWKS_URL_TRUST=FORBIDDEN

A relying party must not trust:
- unsigned claims;
- browser-supplied subject identity;
- caller-supplied Wallet identity;
- unknown `kid`;
- wrong audience;
- wrong issuer;
- expired assertion;
- not-yet-valid assertion;
- sender-binding mismatch.

## Public-key trust model

PUBLIC_TRUST_DISTRIBUTION=READ_ONLY_JWKS  
PRIVATE_SIGNING_KEY_SHARED_WITH_RELYING_PARTY=FORBIDDEN  
SHARED_SYMMETRIC_SIGNING_SECRET=FORBIDDEN

Production and staging do not share signing keys, subject namespaces, or session stores.

## Sender constraint

When sender constraint is required:

DPOP_PROOF_KEY_MATCHES_CNF_JKT=REQUIRED  
CNF_JKT_MISMATCH=DENY  
MISSING_REQUIRED_SENDER_CONSTRAINT=DENY  
CALLER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED_CNF_JKT=FORBIDDEN

`cnf.jkt` is the JWK thumbprint of the key to which the assertion is sender constrained.

Possession of an otherwise valid assertion is insufficient when the contract requires sender constraint.

A sender-constrained assertion presented as a replay outside its valid sender/context must be rejected.
